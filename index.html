Selected work

CRYPTO 2026

Chopin: Optimal Pairing-Based Multilinear Polynomial Commitments from Bivariate KZG

With Pavel Hubáček, Aleksi Kalsta, and Kristýna Mašková. We construct a pairing-based multilinear polynomial commitment from bivariate KZG that achieves optimal prover work, verification time, and proof size, together with knowledge soundness in the standard model.

Video presentation

CRYPTO 2025

On Extractability of the KZG Family of Polynomial Commitment Schemes

With Pavel Dvořák, Charlotte Hoffmann, Pavel Hubáček, Kristýna Mašková, and Martin Pastyřík. A unifying framework for the knowledge soundness of univariate and multivariate KZG-like polynomial commitments, including the first standard-model extractability proofs for multivariate KZG and several variants under falsifiable assumptions.

Video presentation (starts at 18:45)

Ongoing research

On Lifting AGM Security to AGM with Oblivious Sampling

With Pavel Hubáček and Dominik Stejskal. We develop a general framework for lifting security proofs from the algebraic group model to the AGM with oblivious sampling, extending the approach to interactive games and demonstrating it on concrete cryptographic primitives.

Ongoing research

Between SDH and RSDH: Towards the Exact Hardness of KZG Special Soundness

With Pavel Hubáček and Kristýna Mašková. We pinpoint the hardness of standard KZG special soundness between SDH and ARSDH: a new standard-model proof reduces it to fixed-set RSDH, while generic black-box separations show that each step of the resulting hierarchy is strict.

Research interests
